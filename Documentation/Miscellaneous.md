# MISCELLANEOUS
## Custom Greeting Text

Overrides the greeting phrase shown on your own player card. 

**NOTE: Client side only**

## Hide user ID

Visually hides your UID so they don't appear in videos of screenshots 

**NOTE: Client side only, this will not hide your UID from ProjectMoon's servers.**

## Disable Liquid Glass

Disables the tweak's liquid glass, replacing it with a blur-based UI design

requires a restart of the app before it takes effect.
